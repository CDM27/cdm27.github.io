Submission Instructions

We invite original research on controllable data memorization. Shorter position and work-in-progress submissions are also planned, subject to the final TRUST publication policy.

Manuscript Requirements

Submission categories, page limits, the manuscript template, and anonymity requirements will be announced once confirmed.

Review and Selection

Each research submission will receive at least two independent reviews, with an additional review requested when needed. Selection will consider relevance to CDM, technical or empirical soundness, clarity of assumptions and claims, supporting evidence, significance for trustworthy AI, and potential to stimulate workshop discussion.

The workshop will follow ACM and TRUST conflict-of-interest policies. Organizer-authored submissions, if permitted, will be handled only by non-conflicted organizers and reviewers.
