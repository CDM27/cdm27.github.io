Submission Guidelines

We invite original research papers on controllable data memorization for trustworthy AI, including theoretical, empirical, and systems contributions related to our topics of interest. Submissions must present unpublished work and must not substantially overlap with papers already published or concurrently submitted to a journal or conference with proceedings.

Workshop papers follow the same manuscript requirements as the ACM TRUST 2027 main conference:

  • Format: Use the official 2-column ACM SIGCONF template.
  • Page limit: Papers must not exceed 9 pages, including any appendices. References and the GenAI Usage Disclosure section do not count toward this limit and may use additional pages.
  • Anonymous submission: Papers must be prepared for double-blind review. Remove author names, affiliations, and acknowledgments, and write self-citations in the third person.
  • File format: Submit a PDF with embedded fonts, correct margins, and readable figures.

Please consult the TRUST 2027 Authors Guidelines for the full manuscript requirements. Submissions exceeding the page limit will not be reviewed.

Review and Selection

Each research submission will receive at least two independent reviews, with an additional review requested when needed. Selection will consider relevance to CDM, technical or empirical soundness, clarity of assumptions and claims, supporting evidence, significance for trustworthy AI, and potential to stimulate workshop discussion.

The workshop will follow ACM and TRUST conflict-of-interest policies. Organizer-authored submissions, if permitted, will be handled only by non-conflicted organizers and reviewers.

Publication and Presentation

Accepted workshop papers will be considered for publication in the TRUST 2027 conference proceedings. Papers selected for inclusion in the proceedings may undergo an additional light review to determine their suitability for publication.

At least one author of each accepted paper must register and present the work at the workshop. Authors are responsible for obtaining any necessary publication clearances and following the applicable ACM publication policies.
