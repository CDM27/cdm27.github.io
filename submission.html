Prepare Your Manuscript

We welcome original research on the workshop topics. Submissions must present unpublished work and must not substantially overlap with papers already published or concurrently submitted to a journal or conference with proceedings.

Workshop papers follow the manuscript requirements of the ACM TRUST 2027 main conference.

  • Template. Use the official two-column ACM SIGCONF template.
  • Page limit. Papers may use up to 9 pages, including appendices. References and the GenAI Usage Disclosure section are excluded from the limit and may use additional pages.
  • Double-blind review. Remove author names, affiliations, and acknowledgments. Write self-citations in the third person. Remove identifying information from the PDF metadata and any supplementary material.
  • PDF. Embed fonts, use the correct margins, and make sure figures are readable.

See the TRUST 2027 Authors Guidelines for the full manuscript requirements. Submissions exceeding the page limit will not be reviewed.

Review and Selection

Each submission sent for review receives at least two independent reviews. An additional review may be requested when needed. Selection will consider relevance to CDM, technical or empirical soundness, clarity of assumptions and claims, supporting evidence, significance for trustworthy AI, and potential to stimulate workshop discussion.

The workshop will follow ACM and TRUST conflict-of-interest policies. Organizer-authored submissions, if permitted, will be handled only by non-conflicted organizers and reviewers.

Publication and Presentation

Accepted workshop papers will be considered for publication in the TRUST 2027 conference proceedings. Papers selected for inclusion in the proceedings may undergo an additional light review to determine their suitability for publication.

At least one author of each accepted paper must register and present the work at the workshop. Authors are responsible for obtaining any necessary publication clearances and following the applicable ACM publication policies.
