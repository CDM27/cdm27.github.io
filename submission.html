Paper Submission

Controllable Data Memorization for Trustworthy AI

Submission Instructions

We plan to solicit original research papers and, subject to the final TRUST publication policy, shorter position or work-in-progress submissions where appropriate.

For the research scope and topics, see the Call for Papers. Submission and notification dates are listed under Important Dates.

Submission Portal

The paper submission portal will be announced.

Manuscript Requirements

Submission categories, page limits, the manuscript template, and anonymity requirements will be announced once confirmed.

Review and Selection

Each research submission will receive at least two independent reviews, with an additional review requested when needed. Selection will consider relevance to CDM, technical or empirical soundness, clarity of assumptions and claims, adequacy of supporting evidence, significance for trustworthy AI, and potential to stimulate substantive workshop discussion.

The workshop will follow ACM and TRUST conflict-of-interest policies. Organizer-authored submissions, if permitted, will be handled only by non-conflicted organizers and reviewers.

Contact

For submission inquiries, please contact cdm27.tpc@gmail.com.
