About the Workshop

Controllable Data Memorization studies how information from data persists in models and shapes what they know, what they reveal, and how they behave. CDM 2027 asks how selectively we can limit, revise, or preserve those effects as models are trained and updated.

That can mean limiting the exposure of sensitive information, reducing reliance on spurious training signals, or retaining useful capabilities across model updates.

Workshop Objectives

  • Understand What Models Retain

    Trace learned information to its sources and measure how it persists and shapes behavior.

  • Develop Methods for Selective Control

    Limit unwanted influences during learning, revise or remove targeted information after training, and preserve useful knowledge and capabilities through updates.

  • Verify Effects and Establish Limits

    Measure intended changes, retained capabilities, and unintended effects. Establish what can be guaranteed, under what conditions, and where control breaks down.

The planned half-day workshop includes invited talks, contributed presentations, and poster discussions.
