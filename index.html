About the Workshop

Learning from data gives AI models useful capabilities. In the same process, models can retain sensitive information or learn to rely on misleading patterns, including those introduced through deliberate manipulation of training data. CDM 2027 brings together research on what models learn and retain, how that information shapes their behavior, and how to control its effects. That can mean limiting unwanted influences during training, incorporating new knowledge while preserving useful capabilities, or revising or removing information a model has already learned. The workshop also covers how to verify what an intervention changed and what it left intact.
