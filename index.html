Welcome

Welcome to CDM 2027, the Workshop on Controllable Data Memorization for Trustworthy AI, held in conjunction with ACM TRUST 2027.

Modern AI systems are repeatedly shaped by data throughout their lifecycle. As models are trained, adapted, updated, and reused, a central question emerges: how can we control what they learn, retain, express, and forget?

CDM brings together researchers working on the mechanisms, algorithms, guarantees, and evaluation methods for understanding and controlling data memorization. We aim to connect perspectives from machine learning, security and privacy, data management, and distributed systems.

Aim and Scope

The workshop focuses on governing how information from data is learned, retained, expressed, and changed. Control may take place during learning, by limiting sensitive or undesirable information and preserving useful knowledge, or after learning, through targeted modification, forgetting, or removal.

We welcome work across model training, adaptation, and post-training modification, with particular interest in:

  • Understanding and measuring memorization: training-data influence, attribution, auditing, and privacy leakage.
  • Limiting undesired memorization: privacy-preserving learning, differential privacy, and resistance to corrupted or adversarial data.
  • Modifying and removing information: machine unlearning, model editing, targeted correction, and selective forgetting.
  • Preserving useful memorization: continual learning, knowledge retention, and mitigation of catastrophic forgetting.
  • Verifying memorization control: evaluation protocols, theoretical guarantees, tradeoffs, and fundamental limits.

Workshop Format

We plan a half-day workshop combining invited talks, oral presentations of accepted papers, and a poster session with discussion. The program will bring together mechanistic understanding, algorithmic control, theoretical guarantees, and empirical validation.
