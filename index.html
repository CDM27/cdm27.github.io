About the Workshop

Controllable Data Memorization concerns how information from data is acquired and retained in learned models, how it shapes model behavior, and how it can be selectively preserved, constrained, modified, or removed. This includes limiting sensitive or harmful information during learning, retaining useful knowledge through model updates, and revising or removing information after training.

CDM 2027 brings together research on memorization and data influence, privacy-preserving and robust learning, machine unlearning, model editing, and continual learning. The common focus is understanding and controlling the information that models retain from data.

Workshop Objectives

CDM welcomes theoretical and empirical work on the mechanisms, methods, and validation of memorization control.

  • Mechanistic Understanding

    Explain how information is acquired and retained by models, how it changes during learning and updating, and how it affects model behavior.

  • Principled Methods

    Develop methods for memorization control and understand why and when they work, linking algorithm design with analysis and empirical evidence.

  • Rigorous Validation and Fundamental Limits

    Evaluate the intended and unintended effects of memorization control, establish what can be verified, and identify failure modes, tradeoffs, and fundamental limits.

The planned half-day workshop combines invited talks, contributed presentations, and poster discussions to support discussion of these questions.
