CDM 2027 · Workshop on

Controllable Data Memorizationfor Trustworthy AI

Co-located with ACM TRUST 2027, Washington DC, USA

March 7–9, 2027

Modern AI systems are repeatedly shaped by data throughout their lifecycle. Information acquired during training can persist through later fine-tuning, adaptation, and deployment, influencing model knowledge, capabilities, and behavior. As models are increasingly updated and reused, understanding memorization alone is no longer sufficient: we also need ways to control what they learn, retain, express, and forget.

Controllable data memorization concerns how information from data is acquired, preserved, limited, modified, or removed. Control may take place during learning, by limiting sensitive or undesirable information, resisting corrupted or adversarial data, and preserving useful knowledge; or after learning, through machine unlearning, model editing, and selective forgetting. Reliable measurement and verification are essential to establishing whether these interventions achieve their intended effects.

CDM brings together researchers from machine learning, security and privacy, data management, and distributed systems to advance the mechanisms, algorithms, guarantees, and evaluation of memorization control. Through invited talks, contributed presentations, and poster discussions, this half-day workshop aims to connect mechanistic understanding with practical methods and identify open problems in managing learned information for trustworthy AI.
